meet the bleep buddies

Options for the floating avatar. Each buddy has five moods, seven colors and three finishes. Pick one per card, then see it in Settings, on the desktop and on the website below.

From dusk bot

Flat pixel critters in single bold colors. Many shapes, one family. Two tall white eyes, nothing else.

From OpenBot

Soft plush shapes with felt texture and tiny eyes. They read as toys, not machines.

From MausBot

One simple shape with a glossy gradient, white eyes and a small smile. Works from 16px to hero size.

Our rules: one simple shape, a face of two eyes, no outlines, bold color, every buddy original to bleep. Moods map to what the droid does today: idle, listening (wake word), thinking, alert (meetings, errors) and happy (done).

Next steps after you pick

  • Settings key: avatar: { kind, color, finish } in settings.rs, default to your pick.
  • Renderer: replace droidSvg.ts with avatars.ts (one SVG builder, shared mood CSS). Droid.tsx already sets the five moods.
  • Small spots: chat panel head, meeting bar, setup screen and menu bar icon use the same builder.
  • Website: swap the droid in the hero, desktop demo, favicon and og image. Retitle "a little droid" copy.
