bleep vs OpenClaw

OpenClaw is an assistant you message from chat apps. bleep is a native Mac app that floats on your desktop and taps you when something needs you. Both are free and open source.

Updated 18 Nov 2026 · by Aviroop Paul, who makes bleep · bleep 0.5.0

Short answer

  • Pick OpenClaw if you want a general assistant you message from your chat apps, with a large plugin ecosystem, on a machine or server you run.
  • Pick bleep if you want a native Mac buddy that watches your mail, calendar and Claude Code or Codex sessions on its own, with no setup beyond one install command.

What OpenClaw does better

  • Reach. You can talk to it from your phone through chat apps, wherever you are.
  • Ecosystem. It is one of the most starred projects on GitHub, with many skills and plugins from its community.
  • Cross platform. It is not tied to macOS.

What bleep does better

  • It is a Mac app, not a server. Install in one line, and it lives on your desktop as a small buddy.
  • Proactive out of the box. Meeting prep, bill reminders and waiting mail work after setup, with quiet hours and a ping limit.
  • Built for coding agents. bleep watches every Claude Code and Codex session and approves the safe asks by fixed rules, with a log.
  • Careful by design. Sending mail, moving events, deleting and running commands always show a card and wait for your yes. Text in your mail is treated as data, not as instructions.

What bleep is

  • Lives on your Mac. A native app with a small Rust core. A floating buddy on every Space. Double-tap ⌥ to call it.
  • Speaks first. It checks your mail, calendar and coding agents on its own, and taps you before a meeting, when a bill is due, when a mail waits on you, or when an agent is stuck.
  • Unblocks your coding agents. It approves the safe Claude Code and Codex asks your rules allow, like tests, lint and file reads. Push, deploy, delete, secrets and money always wait for you.
  • Any model. Your Claude Code plan by default, an API key from Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini, OpenRouter or Groq, or a local model in Ollama or LM Studio.
  • Private. No bleep server, no account, no telemetry. Mail, memory and logs stay in ~/.bleep.
  • Free and open source. MIT license, source on GitHub.

Questions

Is bleep an OpenClaw alternative for Mac?

Yes, for people who want a native Mac assistant rather than a chat bot on a server. bleep floats on your desktop, watches your mail, calendar and coding agents, and taps you when something needs you.

Does bleep work with WhatsApp or Telegram?

No. You talk to bleep in its own chat on your Mac, or by voice with the optional wake word.

Can bleep use the same models as OpenClaw?

Mostly. bleep runs on your Claude Code plan by default, or on an API key from Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini, OpenRouter or Groq, or on a local model in Ollama or LM Studio.
