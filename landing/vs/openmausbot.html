bleep vs OpenMausBot

Both are open source, both reuse the Claude or Codex plan you already have. OpenMausBot gives you a team of agent bots. bleep gives you one buddy that watches your day.

Updated 18 Nov 2026 · by Aviroop Paul, who makes bleep · bleep 0.5.0

Short answer

  • Pick OpenMausBot if you want an open source version of Grok Bot: a team of agent bots, each with its own machine, run on the Claude, Codex or Grok CLIs you already pay for.
  • Pick bleep if you want one buddy on your Mac that watches your day, speaks first, and keeps your coding agents moving.

How they differ

Both use the AI subscriptions you already have, so neither adds a bill. The difference is the job. OpenMausBot gives you workers: you hand a bot a task and it works on its own machine. bleep gives you a watcher: it sits on your desktop, notices what needs you, and handles the small asks so your own agents and your own day keep moving.

What bleep is

  • Lives on your Mac. A native app with a small Rust core. A floating buddy on every Space. Double-tap ⌥ to call it.
  • Speaks first. It checks your mail, calendar and coding agents on its own, and taps you before a meeting, when a bill is due, when a mail waits on you, or when an agent is stuck.
  • Unblocks your coding agents. It approves the safe Claude Code and Codex asks your rules allow, like tests, lint and file reads. Push, deploy, delete, secrets and money always wait for you.
  • Any model. Your Claude Code plan by default, an API key from Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini, OpenRouter or Groq, or a local model in Ollama or LM Studio.
  • Private. No bleep server, no account, no telemetry. Mail, memory and logs stay in ~/.bleep.
  • Free and open source. MIT license, source on GitHub.

Questions

Is bleep a fork of OpenMausBot?

No. bleep is a separate project with its own Rust core, built with Tauri 2.

Can I use bleep and OpenMausBot together?

Yes. bleep can watch the Claude Code and Codex sessions on your Mac and approve their safe asks.
