bleep 0.5.0

18 Nov 2026 · Release on GitHub

  • Pick your buddy: six characters plus the classic droid, in any color, with a flat, glossy or plush finish. Your menu bar icon matches. Puff is the new default. Find it in Settings > Avatar.
  • Reminders: ask bleep to remind you. A notification comes on time, with Done and Snooze.
  • Usage: model calls, tokens, cost and a heatmap of when bleep works, in Settings > Usage. Near compaction, the gear in the chat shows a dot.
  • Connectors: turn apps on and off in one place. Google Drive is built in. Add any remote MCP server.
  • Files on this Mac: bleep can find and read your files when you ask, PDFs too. A command always shows you a card first.
  • Offers a connector: ask about an app that is off, and bleep shows its switch right in the chat.
  • New Settings: sections, icons and a cleaner look. The gear in the chat opens it.
  • bleep is open source under the MIT license: https://github.com/AviroopPaul/bleep

bleep 0.4.0

17 Nov 2026 · Release on GitHub

  • Say "hey bleep": the droid wakes, the chat opens, and bleep sends what you say next. "Hey bleep, what is on my calendar today?" in one breath works too. Turn it on in Settings > Voice.
  • On your Mac only: speech to text runs on your Mac (a 66 MB model, downloaded once). Audio is never saved or sent anywhere.
  • Pick your mic and speaker: Auto follows what macOS uses, so AirPods take over when they connect. You can change the wake phrase and how sensitive it is.
  • Spoken replies: bleep can read its answer aloud when you asked by voice, with the voices built into macOS. Turn it on or off, and pick the voice.

bleep 0.3.1

16 Nov 2026 · Release on GitHub

  • Auto-update: bleep checks for a new version every few hours. It installs it in the background and asks you to restart. See Settings > Updates.

bleep 0.3.0

16 Nov 2026 · Release on GitHub

  • Answer your coding agents from bleep: tap a session under Agents > Needs you. You see its chat and what it waits for. Pick an option, type a reply, or tap Approve or Deny, and Claude Code goes on. The terminal still asks too. The first answer wins.
  • Bubbles stay until you close them, and each one is saved to the chat.
  • No false limit note: the rate limit note shows only when a turn is really refused.
  • Chat composer: a send icon and a new placeholder.

After updating: bleep updates its Claude Code hooks when it starts. Restart open Claude Code sessions so they use the new hooks.

bleep 0.2.0

16 Nov 2026 · Release on GitHub

  • Bring your own key: Settings > Model picks what runs bleep: Claude Code, or an API key. Pick a provider (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google Gemini, OpenRouter, Groq, Ollama, LM Studio or any OpenAI-compatible server), then a main and a small model. Test checks the key. Keys stay in the Keychain.
  • Daily spend cap: with an API key, today's spend shows in Settings and Activity. At the cap ($1 by default), check-ins, mail triage and screenshot checks wait until tomorrow. Chat still works.
  • Add any MCP server: Settings > Connectors. Paste its URL and bleep finds out how it signs in (none, a token, or OAuth in your browser). Tools that change things always show a card first.
  • Memory: bleep keeps facts you approve and compacts long chats, so it stays fast.
  • Settings redesign: a list on the left, the page on the right. The window resizes and opens on the display where the droid is.
  • Fewer Keychain prompts: all secrets live in one Keychain item, so an update asks at most once.

After updating: macOS may ask for your login password once per old Keychain item while bleep moves them into the new one. Click Always Allow.

bleep 0.0.1

13 Nov 2026 · Release on GitHub

The first version of bleep.

Install (pick one, in Terminal)

Both check the download against its SHA-256, put bleep in Applications and let macOS open it. Then press ⌘ Space and type bleep. A short setup connects Claude Code, Google and your agents.

What it does

  • Floats on your Mac as a small droid. Double-tap ⌥ Option to call it.
  • Watches your Claude Code and Codex sessions. It shows what waits on you, and can approve safe asks by your rules (off until you turn it on).
  • Reads your Gmail and Google Calendar: morning brief, check-ins, bills, meeting prep. It never sends, moves or deletes anything without your tap.

Needs: Apple silicon, macOS 15 or later, and Claude Code installed.

Downloading the dmg in a browser also works, but macOS blocks the first launch (not notarized). Use System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway once.
