Personal AI assistants for Mac: Grok Bot alternatives and more

Grok Bot made always-on AI assistants popular. Here are the main options in November 2026, what each one is for, and which ones are free and open source.

Updated 18 Nov 2026 · by Aviroop Paul, who makes bleep · bleep 0.5.0

Short answer

  • Want cloud agents that do the work? Grok Bot (paid) or its open source copies, OpenMausBot and rakazo.
  • Want an assistant in your chat apps? OpenClaw.
  • Want a free buddy on your Mac that watches your day and your coding agents? bleep.
  • Only want Claude Code alerts? CC-Beeper or Buddi.

Personal AI assistants

bleep

Mac · free · open source (MIT) · proactive

A small AI buddy that floats on your Mac. It watches Claude Code, Codex, Gmail and Google Calendar, approves the safe agent asks, preps your meetings, catches bills, and taps you only when it matters. Runs on your Claude Code plan, any API key, or a local model.

Grok Bot

Cloud · paid plan · closed source

xAI's persistent AI teammates. Each bot has its own cloud computer, signs in to your apps, and works on multi-step tasks. In beta for SuperGrok Heavy and Cursor Ultra subscribers. bleep vs Grok Bot.

OpenClaw

Self-hosted · free · open source

A popular open source personal assistant you message from your chat apps, with a large plugin ecosystem. bleep vs OpenClaw.

OpenMausBot

Desktop plus VMs · free · open source

An open source Grok Bot alternative. A team of bots, each a Claude, Codex or Grok Build agent with its own machine, on the CLI plans you already pay for. bleep vs OpenMausBot.

OpenBot

Self-hosted · free · open source

Agent "coworkers", each in its own container with a browser, with a policy-gated audit trail. Aimed at teams.

rakazo

Self-hosted · free · open source (Apache-2.0)

Often named the closest open source match to Grok Bot's design: persistent named bots with their own memory, browser and terminal.

Hermes Agent

Self-hosted · free · open source (MIT)

A power-user agent you run yourself, with many compute backends.

Claude Code companions for Mac

Smaller apps that sit next to Claude Code. They tell you when a session needs you. bleep also approves the safe asks for you, and covers your mail and calendar.

bleep

Approves safe asks by rules · Codex too · mail and calendar

Watches every Claude Code and Codex session, approves tests, lint and reads, and holds push, deploy and delete for you.

CC-Beeper

Free · open source (GPL-3.0)

A floating macOS pager for Claude Code with status, alerts and voice replies.

Buddi

Free · open source

A notch companion for Claude Code. Approve or deny tool calls from the notch.

How to choose

  • Where should your data live? On your Mac (bleep, CC-Beeper, Buddi), on a machine you run (OpenClaw, OpenBot, rakazo), or in a vendor cloud (Grok Bot).
  • Do you want a worker or a watcher? Workers take tasks and run for a long time. A watcher notices what needs you and keeps the rest quiet.
  • What do you already pay for? bleep and OpenMausBot reuse your Claude or Codex plan. Grok Bot needs its own plan.

Facts about other tools come from their own pages and public reviews as of November 2026. Something out of date? Open an issue.

Questions

What is the best free alternative to Grok Bot for Mac?

It depends on the job. For a free assistant that runs on your Mac and speaks first about your mail, calendar and coding agents, try bleep. For a free team of agent bots like Grok Bot, try OpenMausBot or rakazo.

Which personal AI assistants are open source?

bleep (MIT), OpenClaw, OpenMausBot, OpenBot, rakazo (Apache-2.0) and Hermes Agent (MIT) are all open source. Grok Bot is closed source.

Which AI assistant keeps my data on my Mac?

bleep has no server and keeps mail, memory and logs in ~/.bleep on your Mac. With a local model in Ollama or LM Studio, nothing leaves your Mac at all.
